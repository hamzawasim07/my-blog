*November 09, 2026 (MLN)*

Gold price in Pakistan increased on Monday, with 24-karat gold being sold at Rs432,336 per tola, up Rs3,200.

Similarly, 24-karat gold per 10-gram was sold at Rs370,658 after a gain of Rs2,744, according to rates shared by the All-Pakistan Gems and Jewelers Sarafa Association (APGJSA).

The price of 22-karat gold was also quoted higher at Rs339,782 per 10-gram.

Similarly, silver prices rose in the domestic market, with 24-karat silver being sold at Rs5,723 per tola (+Rs55) and Rs4,906 per 10-gram (+Rs47).

## Global market

Globally, spot gold traded near $4,098 an ounce, up $42.8 or 1.05% from the previous session, as a decline in oil prices eased inflationary concerns, while investors turned their attention towards key U.S. inflation readings and upcoming signals from the Federal Reserve regarding its monetary policy direction.
