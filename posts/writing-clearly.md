title: "Writing Clearly: Three Habits That Help"
date: "2026-11-05"
excerpt: "Small writing habits that make posts easier to read and easier to finish."
---

Good writing is mostly good editing. A few habits make a large difference.

## 1. Lead with the point

Say what matters in the first paragraph. Readers decide quickly whether to continue, so give them a reason.

## 2. Keep sentences short

Long sentences hide ideas. If a sentence needs two breaths, split it in two.

## 3. Cut before you publish

Read your draft once and remove anything that does not serve the reader. Most posts improve when they get shorter.

> Clear writing is a sign of clear thinking.

Try these on your next post and see how it reads.
